Agenda

One calendar for Google and Outlook, personal and corporate.

Phase 1 in testing — not yet released

What it is

Agenda is an Android app that connects your Google and Outlook calendars — including a personal and a corporate account from the same provider — and shows them in one unified view. When something personal shows up on one calendar, it can automatically block that time on the other with a generic event, without revealing details.

What it will do

  • Phase 0 · done

    Project skeleton

    Multi-module Android architecture (Kotlin, Compose, Hilt, Room) already underway, published on GitHub.

  • Phase 1 · built, in testing

    Unified view

    Google and Outlook sign-in (personal and corporate at once), real calendar sync and the merged agenda view are implemented and building successfully — now being tested against real accounts.

  • Phase 2

    Cross-calendar blocking

    A personal doctor's appointment automatically creates a generic "Busy" block on your corporate calendar, and stays in sync if you edit or cancel it.

  • Phase 3

    Events from email

    Detects appointment confirmations and deadlines in your inbox and suggests adding them to your calendar — always with your approval.

  • Phase 4

    Planner

    Projects and tasks, with due dates that can optionally show up on your calendar.

  • Phase 5–6

    Activity log and reports

    Keep a record of what you actually did, and generate reports filtered by date, account or project.
